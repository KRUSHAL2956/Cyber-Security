================================================================================

VPN PROTOCOLS RESEARCH

OpenVPN:
- Most reliable and widely supported
- Strong encryption (AES-256)
- Slightly slower than WireGuard

WireGuard:
- Newer, faster protocol
- Modern cryptography
- Available in ProtonVPN

IKEv2/IPSec:
- Good for mobile devices
- Fast reconnection
- Less common on desktop

================================================================================

PRIVACY FEATURES

ProtonVPN Offers:
- No-logs policy (audited)
- Swiss jurisdiction (strong privacy laws)
- Kill switch (NetShield)
- DNS leak protection
- Open source client

Free Tier Limitations:
- 3 countries only (USA, Netherlands, Japan)
- Medium speed (lower priority)
- No P2P support
- Basic features only

================================================================================

BEST PRACTICES LEARNED

When to Use VPN:
- Public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels)
- Sensitive browsing (banking, confidential work)
- Privacy from ISP tracking
- Accessing region-specific content (responsibly)

Server Selection:
- Choose geographically close servers for speed
- Verify DNS leak protection after connecting
- Enable kill switch for sensitive activities

What VPN Doesn't Protect:
- Not complete anonymity
- Doesn't protect against malware/phishing
- Trust required in VPN provider
- Websites can still track you via cookies

FINAL VERDICT

ProtonVPN Free is good for:
- Public Wi-Fi protection
- General privacy
- Learning about VPNs
- Unlimited bandwidth

Trade-offs:
- 44% speed reduction
- High latency for distant servers
- Limited server options (3 countries)
- Some streaming services block it

Real-world use case: Perfect for public Wi-Fi; acceptable for general browsing; not ideal for gaming or video calls.
