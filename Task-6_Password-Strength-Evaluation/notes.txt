TASK 6 — NOTES (Password Strength & Best Practices)
Date: 28 October 2025

================================================================================

GOAL

- Understand what makes a password “strong” (practical, not just theoretical)
- Compare length vs. character complexity
- Summarize actionable do’s and don’ts

================================================================================

QUICK THEORY

Strength factors (in practice):
- Length (biggest win) → 14–16+ chars recommended
- Unpredictability (no patterns, no common substitutions like P@ssw0rd!)
- Diversity of character sets (lower, upper, digits, symbols)
- Avoid reuse (unique per site)
- Use MFA (password + something else)

Entropy (rough mental math): H ≈ L × log2(N)
- N ~ 26 (lower), 52 (lower+upper), 62 (+digits), ~90 (+symbols)
- Example: 16 chars, lower+upper+digits → 16 × log2(62) ≈ 16 × 5.95 ≈ 95 bits

================================================================================

ATTACKS (RESEARCH NOTES)

- Brute force: try all combos; mitigated by long length and rate limits
- Dictionary: tries common words/phrases; mitigated by randomness/passphrases
- Credential stuffing: uses breached passwords; mitigated by unique passwords
- Phishing: steals passwords regardless of entropy; mitigated by MFA + awareness

================================================================================

IDEAS I TESTED

- Compare 8 vs 12 vs 16 vs 24 char passwords
- Patterns to avoid: Season2025!, Name@123, Qwerty123!, Company@2025!
- Passphrases: 4–5 unrelated words with separators (e.g., carrot-river-lime-robot)
- Add minimal symbols to passphrases to see impact

================================================================================

PASSCODE SNIPPETS (EXAMPLES I TRIED)

- weakpass (8) → only lower, predictable
- Summer2025! (11) → common pattern, mixed but guessable
- Nvy3r6p9 (8) → random but short
- CoffeeHorse!Train?42 (20) → long mixed; strong
- carrot-river-lime-robot (23) → passphrase (words only) but long
- Carrot-River-Lime-Robot! (25) → passphrase + symbol, very strong

================================================================================

BEST PRACTICES (FINAL)

- Use a password manager → unique, long passwords for every account
- Favor length-first → target 14–16+ chars (or 4–5 random words)
- Add diversity but avoid predictable patterns
- Turn on MFA wherever available
- Never reuse passwords; rotate if a breach occurs
